## Source Context

This document contains cleaned excerpts from Cornell Tech Slack announcements about Startup Studio team formation, Superpowers Survey status updates, team composition rules, exceptions, and Spring Studio application deadlines.

## Cleaned Original Text

Students were added to this channel because, based on the information available at the time, they were still advertising themselves as looking for teammates on the Superpowers Student View with Startup Studio as a top preference.

The channel was intended to support students, not overwhelm them. When students found their team, or if they were added by mistake, they were asked to leave the channel and update their status in the Superpowers Survey so they could be removed from the filtered list.

The announcement emphasized that it is best when everyone is working with the same information. Students were asked to be considerate of the community and resubmit the Superpowers Survey form when their status changed.

## Team Formation Guidance

A few things were highlighted as important.

### 1. Be explicit about what you are looking for

If a team needs someone with strong coding skills, a designer, someone great at research, or a partner who is passionate about a specific problem space, students were told to say that clearly in the channel.

The announcement stated that students who state their needs directly find teams faster.

### 2. Be active

Students were told that finding a team after Thanksgiving is historically very difficult.

The announcement advised students not to wait for someone to pick them. Instead, students were encouraged to take agency and put themselves in a good situation early.

The announcement also noted that the options available to students who do not find a team on their own are rarely ideal.

### 3. Understand the rules

Students were reminded to understand the Spring Studio team composition requirements.

## Startup Studio Team Composition Requirements

Startup Studio teams must follow one of the following structures:

- 4 students from at least 2 degree programs
- 5 students from at least 3 degree programs

## Startup Studio Team Exceptions

In very rare situations, Josh may approve a Startup Studio team of 3 students. However, this exception is only possible if the team has a compelling reason why they are the right team for the problem they are tackling.

If a team wants to request an exception, they must Slack DM Josh as a group before submitting their application.

Students were told to expect that most exception requests will not be approved. If an exception is rejected and the team is unsure what to do next, students were encouraged to reach out for help exploring other options.

## Spring Studio Application Deadline

Spring Studio applications are due by Monday, December 1, at 9:00 AM.

Students were strongly encouraged to submit earlier. All submissions received by Monday, November 24, at 9:00 AM would be processed before Thanksgiving break.

## Closing Guidance

The announcement framed the channel as a way for students to have the best chance of shaping their own path.

Students were encouraged to:

- Use the channel fully
- Ask clearly
- Connect intentionally
- Take ownership of the teaming process
