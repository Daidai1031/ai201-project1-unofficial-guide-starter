## What is your company's vision?

Our vision is to use our scale and expertise to create the exceptional guest experiences and owner returns needed to grow our brands in the industry’s most valuable markets and segments.

## What business unit or team do you represent?

Information Security Services, which is part of the Product and Technology organization.

## What is your group’s vision and/or mission?

To grow a hospitality industry-leading cybersecurity program that our owner, guests, business partners, employees, and shareholders can trust to manage cyber risk and provide superior execution and delivery.

## What are your group’s top objectives/priorities for the coming year?

Building protection and resilience into the business using the NIST-CSF framework (Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover) to understand risks & threats, establish enterprise controls to manage the threats, monitoring and escalation, and enabling the playbook to get back to business-as-usual.

Driving efficient and effective compliance by achieving compliance with required regulations and simplifying processes / reducing the effort and cost required to provide control evidence and identify gaps

Partnering across the IHG organization to bring key product and technology priorities to life using security-by-design principles in everything we do and proving the right level of friction to balance security and end user experience.

## Why should the students apply to work with your team, and what will they learn about working at a BigCo?

We look at many drivers that help shape our security strategy, including technology advances, legal and regulatory changes, internal operational changes, the evolving business strategy of IHG, trending security incidents and events, and current risks and audit findings. This helps us create a roadmap with a focus of continually enhancing our overall security maturity. While we are a cybersecurity organization, IHG is a hospitality business, which means we have to balance what is best for our hotel guests and franchise owners with keeping IHG's systems and data secure. This creates a dynamic and often exciting place to work
