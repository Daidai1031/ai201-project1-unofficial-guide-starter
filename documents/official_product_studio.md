## About This Course

Product Studio is a mandatory course for all master’s students taught only during the fall semester. During the semester, you will work on a team to develop a new tech product or service supported by an Industry Advisor and Studio Coach. You’ll experience first-hand how the product development process works in the real world and begin to build a network within the New York tech community.

## How it Works

Every fall, individuals from startups, companies, and organizations in NYC work with students at Cornell Tech. In Product Studio, you and your classmates will develop and present new products, services and strategies to respond to real world challenges.

- Those interested in becoming an industry advisor complete the “industry advisor” form to share more about themselves.
- The Studio team reviews all industry advisor forms and meets with the advisors if needed for more clarity.
- Dreamteam, a matching algorithm created at Cornell Tech, is used to form student teams and connect them to a clearly defined industry, optimizing for students’ interests and cross-disciplinary skills.
- The student teams conduct research, pitch innovative ideas based on their discoveries in the industry space, de-risk their ideas, and begin to build validated solutions. The teams meet with their Industry Advisor at least four times during the semester to seek expert feedback and domain expertise.
- Teams deliver a final presentation and demo to their industry advisors.

## After Product Studio

After completing Product Studio, students take either Startup Studio, BigCo Studio, or PiTech Impact Studio in their last spring semester. Students also enroll in at least one Studio elective to complement their Spring Studio coursework.
