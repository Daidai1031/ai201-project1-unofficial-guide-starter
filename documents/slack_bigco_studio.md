Students are encouraged to thoughtfully rank their top five choices so teams can be matched with the best fit. The announcement notes that students often have the best experience when they stay open-minded about which company they work with.

The announcement also states that BigCo Studio is not an internship or consulting project. The company’s name or prestige matters less than the learning opportunity. Each BigCo partner offers insight into how large organizations operate and innovate.

Past experience suggests that applications for well-known companies like Google, JPMorgan Chase, and Samsung may be plentiful. Students are encouraged to consider lesser-known companies and rank one or more of them highly if the project fit is strong.

Students are asked to read the information provided by each BigCo partner carefully to inform their decision-making.

## Team Formation Announcement

Students were added to a BigCo Studio teaming channel if they were still listed as looking for teammates and had BigCo Studio as a top preference.

The channel was intended to support students who were still forming teams. Students who had already found a team, or who were added by mistake, were asked to leave the channel and update their status in the Superpowers Survey.

The announcement emphasized that it is important for students to keep their status updated so the community is working with the same information.

## Team Formation Guidance

A few things were highlighted as important:

1. Be explicit about what you’re looking for.

If a team needs someone with strong coding skills, a designer, or someone strong in research, they should say that clearly. Students who state their needs directly tend to find teams faster.

2. Be active.

Finding a team after Thanksgiving has historically been very difficult. Students were advised not to wait for someone to pick them, but to take agency and put themselves in a good situation early.

3. Understand the rules.

For BigCo Studio, teams should include:

- 5 to 6 students
- At least 3 degree programs represented

Spring Studio applications are due Monday, December 1 at 9 AM. Submissions received by Monday, November 24 at 9 AM are processed before Thanksgiving break.

The announcement frames the channel as a way for students to shape their own path, ask clearly, connect intentionally, and take ownership of the teaming process.
