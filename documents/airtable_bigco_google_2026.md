## What is your company's vision?

Google's mission is to organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful. We are helping to pioneer advancements in AI and cloud computing technologies.

## What business unit or team do you represent?

I represent the Office of the Chief Information Security Officer (OCISO). We are a team of security professionals with diverse skill sets, spanning cyber security, risk and compliance, and product security engineering.

## What is your group’s vision and/or mission?

The OCISO vision is to enable the wide-scale adoption of Google Cloud by the world’s largest enterprises, in every vertical and in every market. Our mission is to establish Google Cloud as the most trusted cloud & the most effective partner for security transformation, by positioning and delivering security and compliance capabilities that are a key differentiator, and by demonstrating the opportunity for net operational risk reduction in key industry verticals and geographies.

## What are your group’s top objectives/priorities for the coming year?

My team is looking to streamline the process of Google Cloud service enablement through a prescriptive program of technological implementation assets and governance strategy. We are also focused on key FSI industry challenges such as fraud detection and AI enabled security.

## Why should the students apply to work with your team, and what will they learn about working at a BigCo?

Our team is a small but mighty organization that has broad oversight of the security of Google Cloud. The financial services team interacts with the most complex and challenging customers, who face regulatory hurdles and compliance when using the cloud. We are working on numerous different facets of AI security and security for AI that are at the cutting edge of the Cyber security landscape. You should apply to work for this team if you like the intersection of security, AI, and regulatory compliance and are excited about developing programs or assets to implement these capabilities securely. You will also learn about working at a BigCo that is vastly different from others out there, we blend research and engineering to build some of the world's best products, and foster a culture of inclusion and team cohesion.
