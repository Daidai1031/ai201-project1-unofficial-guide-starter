## What is your company's vision?

We aim to be the most respected financial services firm in the world, serving corporations and individuals.

## What business unit or team do you represent?

We are part of the Chase business, specifically in the Operations organization - leading what we refer to as the Voice Product. Within Chase, we're proud to serve millions of people with a broad range of financial services, including personal banking, credit cards, mortgages, auto financing, investment advice, small business loans and payment processing. Chase Operations is a global business unit that supports Chase customers with everything from checking accounts, debit and credit cards, to car loans and investment products as well as loans and products for small businesses. The group also helps customers by preventing fraud, disputing charges, and providing solutions to customers facing challenging financial situations.
