## Maker Day Logistics

For Maker Day on February 15 and February 16, there was no Thursday kickoff. Students were told to work and practice with their teams.

Snacks were available on Thursday around 5:00 PM. Breakfast was available on Friday in Masters Studio at Bloomberg from 9:00 to 10:00 AM. Happy Hour was scheduled from 4:30 to 6:00 PM in the Tata Lobby.

The Maker Day presentation schedule was set for the rest of the semester.

Each participant was expected to prepare a 5-minute presentation and be ready for 10 minutes of dialogue based on the presentation instructions.

There were no packet submissions as in the Fall. Students were told to make sure their presentations were well rehearsed because the presentation would be one of the few grades they received.

Students were encouraged to add an appendix of work completed so far to their slides to support the 10 minutes of dialogue.

To facilitate peer feedback, each team was asked to prepare a question for the preceding group's presentation. The first group presenting in a block would give a question to the last group presenting.

Because of room technology, students were not permitted to use personal devices to present. Instead, they were asked to prepare a viewable link for their presentation and add it to the provided presentation list. A common computer would be used to pull up presentations from the list.

Students were asked to upload their presentation link at least 10 minutes before their presentation block.

## Alumni Crit

The Alumni Crit was scheduled from 5:30 to 8:45 PM.

Presentations were expected to wrap up by 7:15 PM, followed by a happy hour in the Tata Lobby.

Students were told that it was crucial for all students to be in their assigned rooms by the start of the event.

Students were asked to arrive by 5:30 PM in case technical troubleshooting was needed and so the first team could be plugged in and ready to go by 5:45 PM.

## Class and Spinout Clinic

Startup Studio class was scheduled in person from 5:00 to 7:00 PM.

The class began with the Wheel of Opportunity activity. Teams were asked to be prepared to discuss:

- Competitors
- Why now is the right time
- Innovative ideas to address market needs

Following class, there was a Spinout Clinic. Students were reminded to join the related Spinout Clinic Slack channel to stay updated on the event series.

Attendance at the Spinout Clinic was required to maintain eligibility for Startup Awards. Attendance was taken.

## Class Preparation: Wheel of Opportunity

For a later class session, students were told that class would begin in assigned breakout rooms rather than the Bloomberg auditorium.

Students were asked to prepare for the Wheel of Opportunity activity. They were told to come equipped with:

- A concise product narrative for any solutions their team was considering
- An explanation of the problem they were addressing
- An explanation of how they had validated the problem as real

Because the session included another group activity, attendance was monitored at each team table. Students were reminded to be present and engaged.

## Pitching Class

For a Thursday class from 4:30 to 7:00 PM, the guest speaker was Momo Bi, Partner at Watershed Ventures and Advisory Council Member at Cornell Entrepreneurship.

The Wheel question asked each student to come prepared with an elevator pitch.

The pitch could be only two sentences and should include much of the following:

- Who you are
- What you do
- How you do it
- Who you do it for, meaning the customer
- Why now, or what is different or special
